Church website FAQ

We already have a website. Do we have to start over?

No. With the Website Refresh package I can redesign your current site on the platform you already use, including Finalweb, Wix, WordPress and Squarespace, so you keep your domain and logins.

Who will update the site after it launches?

Your choice. I'll show your team how to make simple updates, or I can handle them for you with the $50/month Care Plan.

Can you set up online giving?

Yes. I connect your site to the giving tool you already use, or help you choose one that fits your church.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your logo, service times, a few photos and a short description of your ministries. I can help write and polish the wording.

Are there other costs?

Your domain name and hosting are usually about $15 to $25 a month, paid directly to the provider so you always own your site. I'll help you choose a good option.

Not sure where to start?

Ask for a free site audit. I'll send a short, plain-English action plan with no pressure to buy.
